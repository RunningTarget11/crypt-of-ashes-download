CRYPT OF ASHES - prototype credits

Engine / code
- three.js r170 (MIT License) - https://threejs.org - bundled into game.js
- All game code, shaders, particle effects, UI, procedural cave generator and sound: written for this prototype.
  (All audio - combat sounds, item pickups, the cave rumble bed and the echoing water drips - is synthesized
  live with the Web Audio API. There are no audio files, so there is nothing to license.)

Character models (CC0 1.0 / public domain - cleared for commercial use) - Quaternius, https://quaternius.com
- "Modular Character Outfits - Fantasy" (Standard/free version, CC0 1.0)
    https://quaternius.com/packs/modularcharacteroutfitsfantasy.html
    Used: Male Ranger outfit (hero; also re-tinted for the Grave Caller, Brute and boss) and Male Peasant
    outfit (Ashen Dead skeletons). Base colour desaturated/darkened, meshes decimated, a low-poly LOD
    built for enemies (dc-src/build-char2.mjs).
- "Universal Base Characters" (Standard/free version, CC0 1.0)
    https://quaternius.com/packs/universalbasecharacters.html
    Used: the Superhero Male head (cut from the full body) on every character.
  Both packs ship License_Standard.txt stating "CC0 1.0 Universal (CC0 1.0) Public Domain Dedication".
  They share the UAL 65-bone skeleton, so the UAL animations below drive them with no retargeting.

Animations (CC0 1.0 / public domain - cleared for commercial use)
- Quaternius "Universal Animation Library" (Standard, CC0)   https://quaternius.itch.io/universal-animation-library
- Quaternius "Universal Animation Library 2" (Standard, CC0) https://quaternius.itch.io/universal-animation-library-2
  Both packs ship a License.txt stating CC0 1.0 (https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).
  Used: the UE-style 65-bone rig and the motion-captured clips
  Sword_Regular_A / B / C (+ _Rec recoveries) = 3-hit combo, Sword_Heavy_Combo, Sword_Dash,
  Sword_Attack, Sword_Idle, Idle_Loop, Walk_Loop, Jog_Fwd_Loop, Sprint_Loop, Hit_Chest, Hit_Head,
  Hit_Knockback, Death01, Spell_Simple_Shoot, Zombie_Idle_Loop, Zombie_Walk_Fwd_Loop, Zombie_Scratch,
  Roll, Interact, Consume, Chest_Open, Shield_OneShot. Finger channels were dropped and replaced by a
  fixed sword grip sampled from Sword_Idle; clips were resampled/compressed for the web.
  Glowing eyes, ember-dissolve deaths and hit flashes are shader effects written for this project.

HUD + inventory art - AI-GENERATED (see FLAG)
- Skill/potion/bag/map icons and the ornate metal bezels (dc-src/build-ui.py)
- Item icons: sword, axe, mace, helm, chest armor, gloves, boots, ring, amulet, gold (ui/it_*.webp)
- Dark leather panel texture (bottom bar + inventory), gilded corner ornament, hooded-knight paperdoll
  art behind the equipment slots (ui/tex_leather, ui/orn_corner, ui/paperdoll; dc-src/build-ui2.py)
