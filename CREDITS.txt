Characters
- "Xbot" rigged character + idle/walk/run animations, from the three.js examples repository
  (examples/models/gltf/Xbot.glb), originally from Adobe Mixamo. NOT CC0: Mixamo characters are
  royalty-free for use inside projects, but before a commercial release these should be replaced
  with your own or purchased character models. All hero/enemy looks (armor, ember cracks, glowing
  eyes, cape) are procedural shaders/meshes layered on top of this one rig.
